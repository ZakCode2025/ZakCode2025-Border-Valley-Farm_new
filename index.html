Welcome to Border Valley Farm

Nestled in the picturesque countryside along the A7 corridor between Edinburgh and Carlisle, Border Valley Farm offers a delightful day out for families, garden enthusiasts, and travelers looking for a high-quality place to stop and recharge.

Explore our local farm shop stocked with fresh produce, enjoy home-baked treats in our cozy cafe, meet friendly farm animals in our petting zoo, or stretch your legs along our scenic walking trails.
