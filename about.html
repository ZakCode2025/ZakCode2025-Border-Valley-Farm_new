Our Story & Heritage

Originally established as a working family farm in the heart of the Scottish Borders, Border Valley Farm has transformed into a vibrant eco-friendly visitor destination. Our mission is to combine rural heritage with sustainable tourism, serving both local residents and travelers on the A7 corridor.

We pride ourselves on community focus, supporting regional artisan producers, preserving wildlife habitats, and creating a fun educational environment for visitors of all ages.
