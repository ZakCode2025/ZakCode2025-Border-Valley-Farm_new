Meet Our Animals & Play Outdoors

Children and adults alike will love getting up close with our gentle farm animals. From pygmy goats and Shetland ponies to alpaca, rabbits, and ducklings, supervised feeding and petting sessions take place throughout the day.

Next to the animal enclosures is our outdoor wooden Play Park, featuring swings, climbing towers, zip-lines, and picnic benches for parents to relax.
